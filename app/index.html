SortSense

Point, scan, sort right. Find out in seconds whether something is recyclable, which bin it goes in, and what to do with the parts that are not.

Scan a barcode

Type the barcode number from the pack, or use your camera if your browser supports it. We look the product up in Open Food Facts, the open product database, and break down every part of the packaging.

Logging is an estimate. It uses the weight and material of each recyclable part, and simplified EPA WARM style factors for CO2 avoided.

Photo check (beta)

Take a photo of an item and SortSense will guess what it is made of. The model runs entirely on your device, nothing is uploaded. It is a best guess, so always double check with the guide below.

Model loads on first use, about a few seconds.

What goes where

Search everyday items. Rules vary by city, so this is a general guide. When in doubt, check your city recycling page.

    Why this matters

    About a quarter of what people put in recycling bins does not belong there. One greasy box or plastic bag can send a whole truckload to landfill instead. Sorting right is the cheapest climate action there is: it costs nothing, takes seconds, and keeps real materials in use.

    Sources: Open Food Facts for product and packaging data; impact factors are simplified estimates based on US EPA WARM. Packaging rules differ by city, always check local guidance for the final word.
